# Domain artwork

Original deterministic vector compositions created for ACM OCS'27. No generated
illustrations or borrowed game characters are included. Rebuild with
`apps/web/scripts/build-domain-art.mjs`.

Tool marks are vendored from Devicon (MIT) and Simple Icons (CC0). Exact source
URLs are recorded in `logos/sources.json`; upstream licenses are included in the
same directory. Brand names and marks remain the property of their owners and
are used to identify the tools. No endorsement is implied.
